# ME Tree Beacon

Place the beacon on solid ground and plant saplings within its ring. When they grow, the beacon captures the trees and continually harvests wood and other drops. Replant saplings when the ghost trees disappear.

Connect it to a powered ME network with an available channel to store harvested items automatically. If the network is offline or full, items that cannot be stored drop near the beacon.
