# ME Starlight Infuser

Build the regular Starlight Infuser structure, including its Liquid Starlight pools even when supplying fluid through ME. Connect it to a powered ME network with an available channel.

Insert ingredients by hand, through item pipes or with an ME Export Bus. Infusion starts automatically when its requirements are met. Products enter ME storage; if they cannot be stored, the infuser keeps them and waits before starting another recipe.

Liquid Starlight is drawn from **nearby Chalices → ME storage → pools**, in that order.
