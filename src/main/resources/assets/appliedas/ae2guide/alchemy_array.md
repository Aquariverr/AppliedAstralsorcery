# ME Lumen Alchemy Array

Consumes lumen, Liquid Starlight and catalysts from ME to produce the selected recipe's lumen.

<Recipe id="appliedas:me_lumen_alchemy_array" />

Connect ME to the **bottom**, with power and one channel. Select the output with the screen's arrows, stock the recipe ingredients in ME and enable **Resupply**.

Reserve, Export, Redstone and Filaments work as described for the [ME Lumen Array](lumen_array.md). Recipe lumen is taken directly from ME; nearby arrays are not needed to supply it.
