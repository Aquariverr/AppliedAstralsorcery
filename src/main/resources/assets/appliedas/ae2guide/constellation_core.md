# Constellation Core

A Constellation Core is crafted on an **Iridescent Crafting Altar** and used to craft the [Altar Automation Interface](altar.md).

Fill the **12 positions in the outer corner groups** with crystals attuned to **all 12 different constellations**. Each slot accepts either an **Attuned Rock Crystal or Attuned Celestial Crystal** of its specified constellation, regardless of size, purity or cutting. Duplicate constellations and unattuned crystals do not substitute for a required constellation. Hover over the crystals below to inspect them; constellation names follow the player's research progress.

Place **4 Illumination Powder** at the outer cardinal positions, **4 Aberrant Processors** at the inner corners, and **4 Resonating Gems** at the inner cardinal positions. In the central grid, fill the top and bottom rows with **6 Stellar Filaments**, place **2 Stardust** on the middle left and right, and put **1 Aberrant Crystal** in the center.

Also supply **2 B (2,000 mB) Liquid Starlight, 2 additional Stardust, and 600 Lm of Aion lumen**. The dropped Stardust is separate from the central grid: each craft consumes **4 Stardust in total**. Start at **night** with a Resonating Wand and drop the additional Stardust near the altar. Crafting takes **5 seconds** once all materials are available.
