# Applied Astralsorcery

Applied Astralsorcery connects AE2 with Astral Sorcery, allowing ME networks to store and transfer lumen. Lumen is measured in **Lm** and uses the same icons in ME terminals as in Astral Sorcery's Tome.

## Getting started

1. Craft a [1k ME Lumen Storage Cell](storage.md) and place it in an ME Drive or ME Chest. Each unenhanced cell can store up to **5 lumen types**.
2. Connect your devices with ME cables, power the network, and make sure each device has an available ME channel.
3. Use an [ME Import Bus](buses.md) to extract lumen from a Lumen Array, or an [ME Lumen Filament](filament.md) to collect it from nearby arrays.
4. Check your stored lumen in an ME Terminal. Use an ME Export Bus when you need to supply lumen to an array.
