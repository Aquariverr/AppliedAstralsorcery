# ME Chalice

Supplies nearby devices with fluid from ME and can return surplus fluid to the network.

<Recipe id="appliedas:me_chalice" />

## Usage

1. Connect ME from **above or below**, with power and one channel.
2. **Right-click with an empty hand** to open the screen. Drag a fluid from JEI, or pick up a fluid container with your cursor and right-click the marker to select its fluid.
3. Set the **reserve** (0–64,000 mB). **Pull from ME** replenishes shortages; **Export** returns surplus fluid.

Empty the previous fluid before switching types. Enable Export and leave storage space to empty it automatically. Right-click the chalice with a fluid container for manual transfers.
