# ME Chalice

Arrange the ingredients on a **Resonating Crafting Table** as shown. Start at **night** with a Resonating Wand; crafting takes **5 seconds** once all materials are available.

Follow the native Chalice layout, putting **1 Chalice** in the center and **1 ME Interface** above it. Replace the lowest Sooty Marble with **1 Fluix Block**.
