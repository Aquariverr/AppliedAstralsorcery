# Capacity Enhancement

A **256k Lumen Storage Cell** can be enhanced up to **10 times**. Each enhancement doubles capacity and adds one lumen type. At the limit it holds **15 types** with **1024 times** its original capacity.

<Recipe id="appliedas:lumen_storage_cell_256k_artifact_enhance" />

Place the cell in the center of an **Iridescent Crafting Altar** and arrange the shown ingredients. Supply **2,000 mB Liquid Starlight** and **200 Lm of Prismatic lumen**. Start at night with a Resonating Wand, then drop **one [artifact shard](artifact.md) of any type** nearby.

Enhancement preserves stored lumen, partitions, upgrade cards and the cell's name. **Disassembling the cell removes all enhancements.**
